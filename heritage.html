Three Generations of Trust

From River Gravel to Heirloom

For centuries, the riverbeds beneath Sri Lanka's central hills have yielded the world's most coveted sapphires, cat's eyes and rubies. Marco Polo wrote of these island gems in 1292; today the same gravels run beneath Matale — and EVARA GEMS was born beside them.

What began as our grandfather's single trading licence is now a full atelier: licensed buyers in the pits each dawn, master lapidaries cutting for brilliance rather than weight, and goldsmiths setting every stone by hand. Our promise has never changed — nothing leaves this house that we would not wear ourselves.

  • ◆

    Ethical, Traceable Sourcing

    Every rough is bought directly from licensed pits in Elahera, Ratnapura and the Matale district — fully documented from earth to emerald-cut, with fair wages paid at the source.

  • ◆

    Certified Authenticity

    Each stone ships with certification from the National Gem & Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka, plus independent GRS / GIA verification on request.

  • ◆

    Master Craftsmanship

    Our lapidary and goldsmithing atelier cuts for optical performance and sets each gem by hand in 18k gold and platinum.
