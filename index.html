Our Story

Rooted in the Gem Soils of Matale

For centuries, the riverbeds beneath Sri Lanka's central hills have yielded the world's most coveted sapphires, cat's eyes and rubies. EVARA GEMS was born in Matale itself — three generations of gem traders, lapidaries and goldsmiths devoted to one promise: nothing leaves our atelier that we would not wear ourselves.

  • ◆

    Ethical, Traceable Sourcing

    Every rough is bought directly from licensed pits in Elahera, Ratnapura and the Matale district.

  • ◆

    Certified Authenticity

    National Gem & Jewellery Authority licensing plus independent GRS / GIA verification on request.
